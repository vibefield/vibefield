# Fixtures

GV-AUDIT05 adds JSON fixtures under this directory, validated by `contracts/fixture.schema.json`. Mount the shipping view components. Do not copy their markup.

`origin: "synthetic"` requires `syntheticLabel` of at least 12 characters. A synthetic fixture is not a captured session.

The five view ids are `view.list`, `view.rain`, `view.swarm`, `view.spend`, and `view.timeline`.
