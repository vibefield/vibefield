A comment pad holds lines of plain text, one comment to a line, and its words can be marked: written in another pen, highlighted, struck through or underlined. `read_text` gives you every line, and `read_writing` gives them with their marks. `add_comment` adds a line at the foot of the pad; add one comment at a time, and keep each to a single line. To change words already there, `edit_text` them by quoting them exactly; to mark words, `mark_text` them the same way. The lines already on the pad are the user's: change or mark them only when the user asks you to.
